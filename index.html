        How Groundwork chooses

        There's no AI here and nothing leaves your device. Statements are picked with a few simple rules: your mood sets the tone, the moment and your focus areas narrow the list, things you save or mark helpful come up more often, hidden ones never appear, and anything you've seen in the last few days is held back.
